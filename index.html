About

About me

I am a radio astronomer with experience spanning telescope operations, scientific software, pulsar research and time-domain astrophysics. I currently work as a Telescope Operator and data analyst with CSIRO and the Square Kilometre Array Observatory, supporting SKA-Low operations.

Before this, I was an Associate Lecturer at the Curtin Institute of Radio Astronomy, where I worked across research and teaching. My research has included pulsar surveys with the Murchison Widefield Array, compact-object and transient science with the Vera C. Rubin Observatory's Legacy Survey of Space and Time, and multi-wavelength projects connecting SKAO and LSST science.

Research interests

SKA-Low operations & analysis

Telescope operations, observation validation, station performance analysis, automated QA workflows and scientific Python tooling.

Analysed Green Bank Telescope high-time-resolution data with PRESTO and Python, achieving substantially improved sensitivity over previous surveys.
